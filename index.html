Folio for Canvas

Your course files, saved to your folders.

Pick a folder for each of your Canvas courses. From then on, whenever you open Canvas, new lecture slides, readings, and handouts are saved into it, sorted into subfolders that match the course's modules.

Add to Chrome Add to Firefox

The Chrome version also works in Edge, Brave, and Arc.

How it works

  • Uses your normal Canvas login. No passwords, tokens, or accounts to set up.
  • Finds files even when an instructor hides the Files tab.
  • Never changes or deletes anything already in your folders. Revised files are saved beside the original.
  • Nothing leaves your computer.

Put the folders in iCloud Drive or Google Drive and your course files show up on your iPad too.
